# Orbitron Tactics — Privacy Policy

_Last updated: [DATE]_

Orbitron Tactics ("the app") is made by [DEVELOPER NAME] ("we"). This policy
explains what the app collects, why, and what you can do about it.

**Summary:** no ads, no tracking across other apps or websites, no selling of
data. We collect only what online play, crash fixing and the store need.

## What stays on your device

Fleet progress (credits, upgrades, maneuvers, win/loss record), your fleet
choice, sound setting and which tutorials you have seen are stored only on
your device. Uninstalling the app deletes them.

## What the app sends

| Data | When | Why | Kept |
|---|---|---|---|
| A random player id (anonymous sign-in) | Online play and product events | Lets two players own a game; no email, phone or password is involved | Until you ask us to delete it |
| The nickname you type in the lobby | When you open or join an online game | Shown to your opponent | Deleted with the game, at most one day later |
| Moves and battle events | During an online game | Sent to your opponent in real time; not stored | Not kept |
| Product events: app opened, game started or finished (mode, difficulty, result), store opened, purchase started, finished, cancelled or failed, restore; with app version and platform | While you use the app | To see which modes people play and whether the store works | One year |
| Crash reports: error, stack trace, device model, OS version, app version, the last product events | When the app crashes or hits an error | To fix bugs | At most 90 days |
| Purchase records: product id, store transaction, an anonymous app user id | When you buy or restore | To check purchases and give you what you bought | As long as the purchase is valid |

Our service providers process this data for us:

- **Supabase** (database, online play, anonymous sign-in) — supabase.com/privacy
- **Sentry** (crash reports) — sentry.io/privacy
- **RevenueCat** (purchase checking) — revenuecat.com/privacy
- **Google Play** and the **Apple App Store** handle payments; we never see
  your payment details.

As with any internet service, these providers see your device's IP address
when the app connects. We do not use it to identify you.

Data is encrypted in transit (HTTPS).

## Legal basis (EU/EEA)

Online play and purchases: performance of the service you asked for.
Product events and crash reports: our legitimate interest in keeping the
app working and improving it; they contain no name, email or device
advertising id.

## Children

The app has no chat and no ads. It is not directed at children under 13
and we do not knowingly collect their personal data.

## Your choices and rights

- Play offline (hot seat, single player) and nothing but crash reports and
  product events leaves the device.
- Ask for a copy or deletion of the data tied to your player id by writing
  to [CONTACT EMAIL]. In the email, say roughly when you played online; we
  will find and delete the data within 30 days.
- You may complain to your data protection authority.
