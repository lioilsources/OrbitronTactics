Frequently asked questions

I bought something on a new phone and it is locked.

Open the Comcenter (top right in the lobby), go to the Store tab and tap Restore purchases. Use the Apple ID or Google account you bought it with.

My credits and upgrades are gone.

Fleet progress is stored only on your device; uninstalling the game removes it. Purchases come back with Restore purchases.

Do I need an internet connection?

Only for online games and the store. Single player and two players on one device work offline.

How do I delete my data?

Email us roughly when you played online; see the Privacy Policy.
