Orbitron2D is a strategy game for two that plays like chess and fights like an arcade shooter.

THREE WAYS TO WIN
• Hold all five power fields at the end of your turn
• Capture both the enemy King and Queen
• Slip a Pawn through to the enemy's back rank

PIECES WITH A TWIST
Pawns strike diagonally and never step straight ahead. Bishops snipe over the front line. Knights are blocked by a Rook right in front of them. Lose your King or Queen and the survivor becomes the Last Warrior.

EVERY CAPTURE IS A BATTLE
The two pieces become ships and fight it out in a real-time arena. Dodge, climb and dive; a shot only hits a ship at its own altitude. Raise your shield, spend energy and draw gestures to fly maneuvers: loops, barrel rolls, dashes and a signature move for every ship. After 45 seconds sudden death kicks in, so no duel drags on.

PLAY YOUR WAY
• Single player against an on-device AI on Easy, Medium or Hard
• Two players on one device
• Online and local WiFi multiplayer

BUILD YOUR FLEET
Win battles to earn credits, upgrade your units and learn new maneuvers. Pick your fleet and one of many board piece sets in the Comcenter. A short how-to-play guide shows the rules on first launch.

The whole game is free, with no ads and no account. Optional one-time purchases add premium fleets, maneuver packs and a retro chiptune soundtrack. Restore purchases is in the Store tab.

The game is in English and Czech.
